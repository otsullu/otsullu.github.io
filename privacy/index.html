Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

OTS Ullu (otsullu.com) is an educational site about options income investing. You can read everything on it without an account. This page explains the little we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Reading, views and ratings

  • To count views and unique visitors, and to remember your 👍/👎, your browser stores a random ID. It contains no name or personal information.
  • For rate limiting, your IP address is converted into a salted, one-way hash. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • We use no advertising, no cross-site tracking and no analytics cookies.

Signing in to comment

  • Commenting requires signing in with Google or Microsoft. From them we receive your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your display name (which you can change) and photo appear publicly with your comments. Your email address is never shown.
  • Comments and replies are public. We keep a private record of every edit and deletion so we can moderate abuse, threats and spam.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Resend sends OTS Ullu a notification email when a comment is posted.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website. Videos, podcasts and fonts load from YouTube, Spotify and Google Fonts, under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Removing your data

You can edit or delete your own comments at any time. To have your account and all associated data removed, email otsullu-support@googlegroups.com from the address you signed in with.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
