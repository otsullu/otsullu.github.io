OTS Ullu MVP-1

Three strategies.
One disciplined system.

Short Covered Call

Retain your underlying stock while theta decay works in your favour. Premium collected at open erodes daily — the position closes profitably as the option approaches worthless at expiry.

Short Cash-Secured Put

Sell the put, collect premium upfront, and let theta decay do the work. The engine targets strikes where time erosion outpaces price risk, generating income while actively avoiding unwanted assignment.

Wheel Strategy

Coming in MVP-2: the full income cycle unifying Short Cash-Secured Put and Short Covered Call into a continuous, compounding premium engine.

The Platform Under the Hood

Not a tool. A decision architecture.

MVP-1 ships with a fully pre-configured policy engine, calibrated by consensus across four large language models, that a user can understand, tweak, and own. Because we believe investing is not a light switch. It is a gradient. Performance varies across parameters, market regimes, and objectives. The system is built for that reality.

Pre-Calibrated by Multi-LLM Consensus

Default policy values were derived from consensus across four large language models — not a single opinion, not a gut feel. A starting point grounded in collective analytical reasoning.

Built-in Explainability Reports

Every roll recommendation ships with an explainability report — structured data explaining which filters passed, which scored highest, and why a candidate ranked where it did.

Ready-Made Analysis Prompts

Take your live trade data to any AI instantly. The system generates ready-made prompts pre-loaded with your positions, scores, and context. Real-time second opinion, zero friction.

Tweak, Test, Learn

Every parameter has a defined range and step. Tighten delta, shift Days to Expiration, raise Return on Capital thresholds and observe what survives. The system is designed to build your intuition, not replace it.

"Markets are not binary. Performance does not simply switch on or off. It moves across a continuous gradient shaped by risk, time, and changing regimes. We craft systematic engines that respect this math, providing the precision and discipline required to navigate it."
