YouTube Working For You

Get the most out of every OTS Ullu video

CC

Enable Closed Captions

  1. Play any OTS Ullu video
  2. Click the CC button at the bottom of the player
  3. Captions appear in the video's original language

💡 Works on desktop, mobile app, and TV.

🌐

Change Caption Language

  1. Click the Settings ⚙ gear icon
  2. Select Subtitles / CC
  3. Choose Auto-translate
  4. Pick your preferred language (Hindi, English, etc.)

💡 Auto-translate works in 100+ languages.

📱

Captions on Mobile App

  1. Tap the video while playing
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ (top right)
  3. Tap Captions
  4. Select language or Auto-translate

💡 Save your preference — YouTube remembers it.

🐢

Slow Down for Learning

  1. Click Settings ⚙ in the player
  2. Select Playback Speed
  3. Choose 0.75× for complex topics
  4. Use 1.25× to review faster

💡 Keyboard shortcut: < slower / > faster.

🔔

Never Miss a Video

  1. Subscribe to OTS Ullu on YouTube
  2. Click the Bell 🔔 icon next to Subscribe
  3. Select "All" notifications
  4. Allow notifications in your browser/app

💡 New strategy videos drop regularly.

📋

Use Chapters to Jump

  1. Look for timestamps in the video description
  2. Click any timestamp to jump to that topic
  3. Hover over the progress bar to see chapter names
  4. Use arrow keys to skip 5 seconds at a time

💡 Great for revisiting specific strategies.
